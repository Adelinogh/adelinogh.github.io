About Adelino

Adelino Ferreira, AI & Business Consultant, Boston, MA

I built two service businesses in the U.S. from scratch, as the sole owner, and sold both: one for $225K and the other for $800K. I know what it takes to build a business as the owner, because I've done it twice.

Today I work remotely with small business owners across New England to set up AI where it actually saves time, understand the numbers and run the company the right way. My own businesses are my background, not a promise about yours. Every business is different, so we start by looking at yours.

What I focus on

Most owners I talk to don't need another tool. They need three things: AI set up around how the business actually works, a clear view of the numbers in plain English, and someone in their corner after the setup, because the real questions show up in month two, not on day one.

I'm also a certified business broker, so I've seen small businesses from the buyer's side of the table too.

How I work

I work remotely with owners across Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Rhode Island, Connecticut, Vermont and Maine. Everything happens remotely: the free diagnostic, the setup and the ongoing support.

I'm not an accountant and I don't give tax advice. I help owners understand their numbers and I work alongside their CPA.
