Get the business in order: finance, operations, marketing and sales

What it is

We look at how money, work and customers move through your business, and put a simple system behind each one.

  • Finance: a few numbers you review every week, in plain English.
  • Operations: how the work gets done, written down so it runs the same way every time.
  • Marketing and sales: where your customers come from, and what happens to a lead after the first call.

Who it's for

Owners who grew on referrals and now need a system behind the business. Owners who know their revenue, but not what they actually keep.

What you get

  • A short list of numbers you review every week: cash, what's owed to you, what you owe, and your margins. Your margin is what's left from each sale after the direct cost of delivering it.
  • The key tasks in your business written down step by step, so your team can do them without asking you.
  • A clear view of where your customers come from and how each lead gets a follow up.
  • A list of what to fix first, in order.

We are not a CPA firm and do not provide accounting, tax, legal or investment advice. We're glad to work alongside your accountant.

AI, set up right: finance, operations, sales and marketing

What it is

We use AI to help you run the day to day: creating invoices, keeping track of accounts receivable (what customers owe you) and accounts payable (what you owe), answering customer questions and staying in touch with your customers. We automate the repetitive steps and train the AI on how your business runs, across finance, operations, sales, marketing and customer relationships.

The order matters. We start with how your business actually works, pick the few places where AI saves real time, set it up and train your team to use it.

What stays with people

Your accountant still keeps the books and handles your taxes. AI helps you stay on top of invoices and bills during the month. It doesn't do your bookkeeping, file your taxes or make your decisions.

Three more ways we put AI to work

Long contracts, read in full

AI reads complete documents and contracts and highlights the points that matter most, so you spend less time reading and more time deciding.

The limit: It doesn't replace a lawyer, and it isn't legal advice. Anything legal still goes to your attorney.

Floor plans, read for more accurate bids

For government bids, AI helps read the floor plans and organize the details the bid depends on. It also looks up what similar jobs were awarded for in nearby towns, so your numbers start from what's on the plans and what the market has paid.

The limit: It doesn't win the bid or set your price. You review the numbers and make the call.

Local prices, checked before you quote

AI helps you see what that type of work costs in your area, so your quotes and pricing start from real numbers, not guesses.

The limit: It doesn't decide your price. Your costs and your margin do, and we help you read both.

Who it's for

Owners who keep hearing about AI and want it set up right, not just installed. Owners who tried a tool or two and saw it fade without a setup built around how the business runs.

What you get

  • AI set up in the areas we agree on, around your processes, with tools that fit your business.
  • Training for you and your team on the setup (see AI training below).
  • A plain English list of what the AI does, what it doesn't do, and who on your team checks its work.

Every setup ends with training, so you and your team actually use it. And where AI doesn't fit your business yet, we'll tell you.

AI training for you and your team

What it is

Practical training on the AI tools in your business, using your own work: your invoices, your customer emails, your quotes. You learn how to ask for what you need, how to check the answer, and what's safe to share with an AI tool and what isn't.

Who it's for

Teams that tried AI and it didn't stick. Owners who want to use AI themselves before handing it to the team.

What you get

  • Training sessions for you and your team, built around your own tasks.
  • Simple written steps for the tasks you use AI for most.
  • Help with the questions that come up once you start using it day to day.

Someone in your corner: ongoing business management support

What it is

After the setup, you still have an advisor who knows your business. Ongoing support so the system keeps working and you always have someone to ask. We look at your numbers with you, adjust the AI setup and your processes as the business changes, and talk through the big calls, like hiring, pricing or taking on a new contract.

Who it's for

Owners who have no one to ask when a big decision lands. Owners who want a business that doesn't wait on them for every decision.

What you get

  • Regular reviews of your numbers and your systems.
  • An advisor to call on the big decisions.
  • Changes to your AI setup and processes as the business grows.

Put together, the work adds up to one thing: a business that runs without you in the room for every decision. That makes Monday morning easier. And if you ever decide to sell, it matters even more. A buyer doesn't pay for how busy you are. They pay for what the business earns when it doesn't depend on you. We don't promise a sale, a buyer or a price.

Found on Google and in AI answers: SEO and GEO

What it is

We use AI in our SEO and GEO work, so the right people have a better chance of finding your business on Google and in answers from tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.

In practice, that means making sure your website and your business details tell Google and AI tools clearly what you do and where you work, and adding content that answers the questions your customers already ask.

Who it's for

Owners whose customers come mostly from referrals and word of mouth, and who want more of them to come from search.

What you get

  • A review of how your business shows up on Google and in AI answers today.
  • A plan for what to fix first and what to publish.

The limit: No one can promise a ranking or a number of leads. AI speeds up the work; your offer and how fast you reply still matter.

Questions about our services

Are you an accountant? Do you replace my CPA?

No. We are not a CPA firm and do not provide accounting, tax, legal or investment advice. We're glad to work alongside your accountant. Our part is helping you read your numbers and decide what to do next.

Do you do bookkeeping or file taxes?

No. Your bookkeeper and your accountant keep the books and file your taxes. AI helps you keep track of invoices and bills during the month, and we help you read the numbers and decide what to do next. If you don't have an accountant yet, we'll point you to the right kind of professional.

Is the contract reading legal advice?

No. AI reads long documents and contracts and highlights the points that matter most, so you get to a decision faster. It doesn't replace a lawyer, and we don't give legal advice. For legal questions, talk to your attorney.

Do you only work in Boston?

We're based in Boston and work with small business owners across New England. Everything happens remotely: the free diagnostic, the setup and the ongoing support.

How much does it cost after the diagnostic?

The diagnostic is free, with no obligation. If you'd like help after it, the cost depends on what we find and what you want to set up. We'll walk you through a proposal before you decide anything.
