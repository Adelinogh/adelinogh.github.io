What AI actually does for a small business

Four ways we put AI to work, set up around how your business runs. Each one comes with a clear limit.

Long contracts, read in full

AI reads complete documents and contracts and highlights the points that matter most, so you spend less time reading and more time deciding.

The limit: It doesn't replace a lawyer, and it isn't legal advice. Anything legal still goes to your attorney.

Floor plans, read for more accurate bids

For government bids, AI helps read the floor plans and organize the details the bid depends on. It also looks up what similar jobs were awarded for in nearby towns, so your numbers start from what's on the plans and what the market has paid.

The limit: It doesn't win the bid or set your price. You review the numbers and make the call.

Local prices, checked before you quote

AI helps you see what that type of work costs in your area, so your quotes and pricing start from real numbers, not guesses.

The limit: It doesn't decide your price. Your costs and your margin do, and we help you read both.

Found on Google and in AI answers

We use AI in our SEO and GEO work, so the right people have a better chance of finding your business on Google and in answers from tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.

The limit: No one can promise a ranking or a number of leads. AI speeds up the work; your offer and how fast you reply still matter.

Every setup ends with training, so you and your team actually use it. And where AI doesn't fit your business yet, we'll tell you.

Questions owners ask

What does a small business advisor do?

In practice, a small business advisor helps the owner run the company well: read the numbers, organize how the work gets done, and make the big decisions with someone who knows the business. With us, that also means setting up AI where it saves real time and training you and your team to use it.

What happens in the free diagnostic?

It's a 30 minute online consultation with Adelino. He'll ask about four areas: your numbers, how the work gets done, how customers find you and buy from you, and where AI is or isn't helping today. In the last part of the call, he'll tell you what he sees and the first three things he'd fix. If it makes sense to talk about working together, he'll ask. If it doesn't, he'll tell you that too. Within 24 hours, you'll get a short written summary by email.

Are you an accountant? Do you replace my CPA?

No. We are not a CPA firm and do not provide accounting, tax, legal or investment advice. We're glad to work alongside your accountant. Our part is helping you read your numbers and decide what to do next.

Do you only work in Boston?

We're based in Boston and work with small business owners across New England. Everything happens remotely: the free diagnostic, the setup and the ongoing support.

Do I need to be organized before using AI?

No. That's what the diagnostic is for. AI works best on a task that already gets done the same way each time. If that process isn't there yet, we set it up first. The tool comes second.

Do I have to be planning to sell my business?

No. Most of this work is about running the business well today: numbers you understand, AI where it helps, and a company that doesn't wait on you for every decision. If you ever decide to sell, those same things help. We don't promise a sale, a buyer or a price.

Is the contract reading legal advice?

No. AI reads long documents and contracts and highlights the points that matter most, so you get to a decision faster. It doesn't replace a lawyer, and we don't give legal advice. For legal questions, talk to your attorney.

Can AI help with government bids?

Yes, in two ways. It helps read the floor plans, so your bid starts from what's actually on the plans. And it looks up what similar jobs were awarded for in nearby towns, so you can see where past prices landed. It doesn't win the bid and it doesn't set your price. You still make the call.

How much does it cost after the diagnostic?

The diagnostic is free, with no obligation. If you'd like help after it, the cost depends on what we find and what you want to set up. We'll walk you through a proposal before you decide anything.

How long until I see results?

It depends on your business and on what we find in the diagnostic, so we don't promise a timeline before we look. What we can tell you at the end of the call is what we'd fix first, and why.
