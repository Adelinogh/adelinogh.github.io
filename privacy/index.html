Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This page explains, in plain English, what information we collect on askadelino.com, why we collect it and what we do with it. We follow applicable laws.

Who we are

This website is run by Prosperite Investments and Consulting LLC, doing business as Consulting Services, a small business advisory based in Boston, Massachusetts, in the United States. We work with clients remotely. When this policy says "we" or "us," it means Prosperite Investments and Consulting LLC.

Questions about your privacy: adelino@askadelino.com

What we collect when you book a call

When you book The Run It Right Diagnostic, the booking form asks for:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • Your business name and website
  • What your business does, and its town and state
  • Your role in the business
  • How many people work in the business
  • A rough range of annual revenue (you can choose "Prefer not to say")
  • The area you most want to get in order first
  • What you'd like to walk away with from the call

Some links to this page carry tracking tags called UTM parameters. They are short labels in the web address that show where a visit came from, such as a LinkedIn post or an email. If your link had them, they are saved with your booking.

We don't send text messages.

How we use it

We use this information to:

  • Prepare for your diagnostic consultation and hold it online
  • Send you a short written summary by email within 24 hours after the call
  • Reply to you and follow up on our conversation
  • Understand where our bookings come from
  • Measure our ads and show them to people who have visited this site

We don't record calls

Diagnostic calls are not recorded. Automatic recording is turned off.

The services we use

A small number of services run the site and the calls for us. Each one handles your data under its own privacy policy.

  • Calendly runs the booking calendar on this page. Your booking details go to Calendly, and Calendly may use cookies and collect technical data. Calendly's privacy policy
  • Google Workspace runs our email and calendar. Your booking and our emails with you are kept there. Google's privacy policy
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub records technical data about visits, such as your IP address, for security. GitHub's privacy statement
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on this page. To load them, your browser connects to Google, and Google receives technical data such as your IP address.
  • Meta Platforms runs our ads on Facebook and Instagram and the Meta Pixel described below. Meta's privacy policy

Cookies, ads and measurement

We advertise on Facebook and Instagram. To see which ads bring visits and bookings, and to show our ads to people who have visited this site, we use the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms, on askadelino.com.

The Meta Pixel sets cookies and collects technical data, such as the pages you visit on this site, the ad you came from, the fact that a booking was completed, your IP address and information about your browser and device. Meta may connect this data to your Facebook or Instagram account, under its own privacy policy.

We don't send Meta your name, your email or your answers to the booking form. The Calendly calendar may set its own cookies, as its privacy policy explains.

Your choices: you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage the ads you see in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, this site doesn't load the Meta Pixel.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information for money. Apart from the ad measurement described in "Cookies, ads and measurement," we share it only with the services listed above, so they can do their part for us, and when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your booking details while we're in conversation with you and for up to 24 months after our last contact, and then we delete them. If you become a client, we keep what we need while we work together, and after that for as long as the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal information we have about you
  • Correct it
  • Delete it

Email adelino@askadelino.com. We may ask you to confirm it's you, and we'll reply within 30 days. If you'd rather not get follow up emails from us, tell us and we'll stop.

Please don't put sensitive information in the booking form, such as Social Security numbers, bank details or health information.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and limit who can see it. No website or email service is perfectly secure, so if you have a concern, write to us.

Children

Our services are for business owners and are not meant for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a minor has booked a call, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may add new tools later, such as one for email newsletters. If we do, we'll update this policy before we start using them. Each update changes the date at the top of this page.
