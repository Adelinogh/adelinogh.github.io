Three short demonstrations of the work I set up for business owners every day. A sample company, sample data, and the owner still makes every call that matters.

Demo 1

Takeoff. Estimate. Invoice.

A bathroom plan comes in as a PDF. AI does the takeoff, builds the estimate from your price list and, when you tell it what to bill, prepares the invoice and the email. You check each one before it goes out.

Demo 2

New lead. Follow up. Supplier bill.

A site visit request gets a reply and a spot on the calendar, inside the rules you set. An estimate with no answer gets a follow up waiting in your Drafts. A supplier bill gets read and entered for you to approve.

Demo 3

126 pages. One bid.

A public bid package gets read cover to cover: the dates, the requirements and the risks, each with the page to check. AI suggests a price from your past jobs and local cost data. You set the final number.
