What AI actually does for a small business

In practice, AI helps you run the day to day. We set it up around how your business works, train it on your processes, and train you and your team to use it.

Where it helps

  • Invoices. Creating invoices and keeping track of who has paid.
  • What's owed, both ways. Accounts receivable, what customers owe you, and accounts payable, what you owe, with reminders before a due date slips by.
  • Customer service. Drafting replies to customer questions for you or your team to review and send.
  • Customer relationships. Keeping track of each customer and drafting the follow up after a job or a quote.
  • The rest of the week. Routine steps in operations, sales and marketing, like reminders, weekly reports and first drafts.

We automate the repetitive steps and train the AI on how your business runs, across finance, operations, sales, marketing and customer relationships.

What stays with people: Your accountant still keeps the books and handles your taxes. AI helps you stay on top of invoices and bills during the month. It doesn't replace your bookkeeper or your CPA, and it doesn't make your decisions. Prices, hires and the big calls are still yours, and we help you make them with real numbers.

We are not a CPA firm and do not provide accounting, tax, legal or investment advice. We're glad to work alongside your accountant.
